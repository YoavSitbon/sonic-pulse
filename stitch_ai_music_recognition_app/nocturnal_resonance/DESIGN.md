## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the precision, atmosphere, and energy of real-time acoustic signal capture. Engineered for high-speed acoustic discovery, spectral analysis, and music curation, the interface treats sound as luminous energy piercing a deep, nocturnal vacuum.

The visual direction merges **sleek futuristic minimalism** with **hyper-polished glassmorphism** and controlled **photonic luminescence**:
- **Deep Immersion:** Backgrounds retreat into absolute nocturnal navy and obsidian slate, eliminating ambient distraction and focusing focus strictly on dynamic sonic data.
- **Acoustic Luminescence:** Key interactive states, audio visualizers, and identification anchors use high-voltage electric indigo and vibrant cyan accents that pulse, glow, and trace soundwaves.
- **Precision Metrics:** Data readouts, harmonic keys, frequencies, and confidence percentages maintain the crisp visual clarity of a high-end audio engineering cockpit while remaining frictionless for casual mobile listeners.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for deep nocturnal OLED contrast, dynamic glowing states, and instantaneous optical hierarchy:

- **Primary (`#6366F1` - Electric Indigo):** The core brand frequency. Drives prime interactive catalysts, core listening states, master play toggles, and primary navigation active markers.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` - Vibrant Cyan):** The waveform signal. Reserved for active sound wave visualization bars, high-frequency spectral spikes, live acoustic detection meters, and match confirmation signals.
- **Tertiary (`#A855F7` - Ultraviolet Resonance):** Used sparingly for harmonic tags, genre gradients, rare audio metadata, and spectral transitions when blending from indigo to cyan.
- **Surfaces & Neutrals:**
  - `Surface Canvas`: `#090D16` (Deep midnight slate)
  - `Surface Container Low`: `#0F172A` (Sub-tier panel base)
  - `Surface Container`: `#111827` (Card and analysis container standard)
  - `Surface Container High`: `#1E293B` (Elevated modals, floating player sheets)
  - `Surface Container Highest`: `#334155` (Hover states, subtle borders)
  - `Text High-Contrast`: `#F8FAFC` (Titles, track names, frequency peaks)
  - `Text Medium-Contrast`: `#94A3B8` (Artist names, metadata labels, time stamps)
  - `Text Subtle`: `#475569` (Inactive tabs, grid lines, disabled indicators)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy bridges high-tech data clarity with modern consumer elegance:

- **Headlines & Track Displays (`Sora`):** Imparts a progressive, sculptured geometric authority. Used for hero track titles, discovery status notifications ("LISTENING...", "MATCH CONFIRMED"), and major section anchors.
- **Body & Editorial Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides soft, humanized, crystal-clear readability for lyrics streams, album biographies, and dense musical credits against dark backdrops.
- **Technical Readouts & Acoustic Labels (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides engineered, monospace-adjacent discipline for frequency metrics (e.g., `44.1 kHz`, `128 BPM`), audio formats (`FLAC`, `DOLBY ATMOS`), pill badges, and timestamp durations.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial model relies on a rhythmic 4px/8px incremental grid, calibrated to sustain comfortable single-thumb operation while supporting dense visual spectrum analytics:

- **Canvas & Gutters:** Built on a fluid 4-column layout on mobile devices with a standard `1.25rem` outer horizontal margin. Content tiles and track list columns separate through uniform `1rem` gutters.
- **Safe Areas & Hero Staging:** The primary recognition trigger resides within an uninterrupted vertical center stage (minimum 280px circular clearance) to accommodate multi-ringed acoustic wave pulses without overlapping adjoining metric cards.
- **Vertical Hierarchy:**
  - Micro distances (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) group paired telemetry (e.g., `BPM` label directly above its numeric metric).
  - Component padding (`space-md`) bounds internal metadata within analysis cards.
  - Section dividers (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) segment discrete modules like "Recent Captures," "Acoustic Fingerprint," and "Streaming Targets."

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids conventional drop shadows, leaning instead into **backlit illumination, translucency, and photonic edge diffraction**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid nocturnal slate (`#090D16`). Deep, non-reflective backdrop.
- **Layer 1 (Recessed/Track Surfaces):** Transparent dark slate fills (`rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.6)`) with `1px` subtle boundary borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- **Layer 2 (Floating Music Analysis Cards):** Backdrop-filtered frosted glass (`backdrop-blur: 16px`, `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.8)`), enclosed by dynamic top-lit gradient borders (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.3) 0%, rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1) 50%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02) 100%)`).
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Sticky Floating Player):** High-density glass (`backdrop-blur: 24px`, `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.85)`) surrounded by a subtle 1px ring (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.2)`).
- **Luminescent Depth (Waveforms & Glowing Elements):**
  - Ambient Core: `0 0 24px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.35)`
  - Active Pulse / High Resonance: `0 0 40px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.45), 0 0 12px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.7)`
  - Outlines avoid standard black drop-shadows; they rely strictly on contrasting light spill against dark ground.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **pill-dominant and continuous-curvature geometry**, creating an intentional contrast between organic fluid soundwaves and sleek cybernetic hardware:

- **Full Capsule Radii (`rounded-full`):** Reserved for interactive pill tags, acoustic status badges, waveform bar caps, scrub handles, and the master circular listening sphere.
- **Surface Panels & Cards (`rounded-2xl` / `1.5rem` to `2rem`):** Containers soften the technical data within, preventing the interface from feeling clinical or stark.
- **Micro Elements (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem`):** Album thumbnail art, spectrum visualizer bins, and segmented controls maintain disciplined soft corners to optimize screen real estate.

## Components

### Hero Audio Recognition Sphere
- **Structure:** Concentric circular pulse rings with an interactive core trigger (min 96px diameter).
- **Resting State:** Subtle electric indigo radial glow with a frosted slate core (`#111827`) housing an animated vector sound wave icon.
- **Active Listening:** Concentric outer SVG rings expand outward with fluid scaling. The inner core radiates with dual cyan-indigo energy (`#06B6D4` to `#6366F1`), responding dynamically to mic input amplitude.

### Audio Waveforms & Spectrum Visualizers
- **Structure:** Array of vertical equalizer bars or circular radial splines.
- **Styling:** Vertical bars feature fully rounded capsule caps (`width: 3px` to `4px`). Gradient vertical fills shift from primary electric indigo at base to vibrant cyan at peak amplitude.
- **Interaction:** Real-time height modulation with neon bloom glow applied to peaks exceeding -6dB.

### Music Analysis Cards
- **Structure:** Translucent container housing album art, track information, spectrogram previews, and acoustic metrics (Key, BPM, Energy, Confidence).
- **Styling:** Frosted dark slate surface (`#111827`/80) with a 1px continuous hairline stroke illuminated subtly from the top edge. 
- **Data Arrangement:** Track metadata set in `Sora` (Headline-sm), supported by a grid of micro data chips displaying acoustic attributes in `Space Grotesk`.

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Full pill radius (`rounded-full`), solid electric indigo fill (`#6366F1`) or vibrant gradient (`#6366F1` to `#06B6D4`). Text set in bold uppercase `Space Grotesk` with high-contrast white typography (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Action:** Full pill radius with frosted slate interior (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`), surrounded by a 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), shifting to cyan border illumination on press.

### Chips & Pill Tags
- **Structure:** Ultra-compact horizontal capsules for genre tags, audio quality labels (`Hi-Res`, `Lossless`), and acoustic metrics (`124 BPM`, `F# Min`).
- **Styling:** High-contrast dark backgrounds (`#1E293B`) framed with low-opacity indigo/cyan borders. Typography set strictly in `label-sm` or `label-md` using `Space Grotesk` with expanded tracking.

### Track Lists & History Rows
- **Structure:** Compact horizontal cards featuring a left-aligned micro album square (`rounded-lg`), middle track details (`Sora` for title, `Plus Jakarta Sans` for artist), and right-aligned match timestamp and context menu trigger.
- **Divider:** Eliminated in favor of an 8px vertical gap between discrete row containers, maintaining modular separation.

### Inputs & Audio Scrubbers
- **Search & Filter Inputs:** Dark recessed capsule fields (`#0F172A`) with subtle inset borders. Glowing cyan caret and search icon.
- **Scrubber Sliders:** 4px track height with a completed segment glowing in vibrant cyan, unfinished in dark slate (`#1E293B`), paired with an 8px circular thumb haloed in electric indigo.
